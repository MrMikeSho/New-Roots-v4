Community Guidelines

Be kind

Treat every parent with respect. No harassment, hate or shaming.

Be honest

Post real needs, real listings and real events. No scams, no fake offers.

Protect children

Never post photos of other people's children, and never share a child's school, address or other identifying details.

Keep contact details safe

Share your phone number only where you are comfortable. Our team may remove posts that put anyone at risk.

In danger?

For emergencies call your local emergency services first, then use our Support page.
