Privacy Policy

Plain-language draft — have it reviewed before launch.

What we collect

When you sign up we receive your name and email (Google sign-in) or your phone number (SMS code). You may add your city, number of children and a short bio. Listings and posts you submit include the name and phone number you type in.

How we use it

To run your account, show approved listings and posts to other members, and keep the community safe. We do not sell your data.

Who can see it

Only what you put in a listing or post is public once approved. Your profile is shown on Member Profiles only if you tick that option.

Where it is stored

Your data is stored with Supabase. Sign-in codes are sent by SMS through our provider.

Your choices

You can edit your profile any time, and ask us to delete your account and posts by contacting the Village team from the Support page.
